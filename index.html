Forward your receipts

A one-time setup in your email. Forward from as many inboxes as you like — your own, a spouse’s, a shared household email — all to the one address below. After this, store receipts sort themselves.

Do it on a computerOpen gmail.com in a web browser on a desktop or laptop — the Gmail phone app can’t create forwarding filters.

Part 1 · Add the forwarding address

Do this once in each inbox you forward from.

1
Open your Gmail settingsIn Gmail: the Settings gear (top-right) → See all settings.
2
Open the Forwarding tab & add the addressGo to the Forwarding and POP/IMAP tab, then click Add a forwarding address and paste the address below. Then Next → verify your Google account login and Send verification code → Proceed.
categoraize+yourname@gmail.com
Then wait about a minuteGmail sends a confirmation we handle for you automatically — please wait while we verify and accept your forwarding. We’ll email you when it’s ready; then refresh Gmail and do Part 2.
Part 2 · Create the filter

After our “verified” email arrives, refresh Gmail and do this in the same inbox.

3
Start a new filterSettings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter.
4
Paste this into “From,” then Create filter
from:(amazon.com OR target.com)
5
Forward to your address → Create filterTick Forward it to and choose your CategorAIze address. Optional: also tick Archive it (keeps it out of your inbox) or Mark as read. Then click the big blue Create filter button.
